; alt-gate-mixed -- QUIRKS #17: only ALTITUDE indications wait for the
; ground-elevation announcement; non-altitude speech does not.
;
; The track (gen_jump --vacc-poor 70) reports vAcc 30 m for the first 70 s
; -- through a short ground roll and the first ~18 s of a fast climb that is
; already ABOVE ALT_MIN (1500 m) -- then vAcc drops to 2 m. So the
; say-altitude latch stays set until ~t=70, when the ground-elevation
; announcement finally plays.
;
; Two rotating speech entries share the queue: Sp_Mode 1 (vertical speed,
; a NON-altitude value) and Sp_Mode 12 (altitude). With Alt_Step set there
; is also an altitude-step announcement source. The B4 pins are:
;   * Sp_Mode 1 speech PLAYS during the poor-vAcc window, before the ground
;     announcement -- non-altitude speech is never gated on the latch.
;   * Sp_Mode 12 (altitude) speech AND the Alt_Step announcements produce
;     NOTHING until AFTER the ground-elevation announcement (~t=70), even
;     though the aircraft is already above ALT_MIN and moving fast enough to
;     satisfy V_Thresh -- altitude indications wait for the confirmation.
;     The gated Sp_Mode 12 slot is SKIPPED (rotation advances), so its turns
;     do not stall the Sp_Mode 1 entry.
;
; Coverage (QUIRKS #20): after vAcc recovers, the fast climb and freefall
; cross Alt_Step boundaries frequently while a periodic utterance is still
; queued, exercising the "Alt_Step crossing skipped because speech is
; pending" branch (audio_control.c:771).
